Hangzhou Travel Guide

Hangzhou is suitable for nature, lake views, tea culture, temples, citywalk and relaxed travel.

Recommended attractions:
West Lake: core attraction, suitable for walking, boating and photography.
Lingyin Temple: suitable for Buddhist culture and quiet sightseeing.
Longjing Village: suitable for tea culture and mountain views.
Hefang Street: suitable for local snacks and old-town atmosphere.
Xixi Wetland: suitable for nature and slow travel.

Food recommendations:
West Lake vinegar fish, Dongpo pork, Longjing shrimp, Pian Er Chuan noodles and local pastries.

Suggested route:
Day 1: West Lake, Broken Bridge, Su Causeway, evening lake view.
Day 2: Lingyin Temple, Longjing Village, tea culture experience.
Day 3: Xixi Wetland, Hefang Street, local food.

Travel tips:
West Lake area is large. A relaxed itinerary should focus on fewer spots each day.
