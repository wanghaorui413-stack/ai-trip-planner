Shanghai Travel Guide

Shanghai is suitable for citywalk, food, museums, shopping, architecture and night views.

Recommended attractions:
The Bund: best for night views and historical architecture. Recommended in the evening.
Lujiazui: best for modern skyline and city photography.
Wukang Road: suitable for citywalk, cafes and street photography.
Yu Garden: suitable for traditional architecture and local snacks.
Shanghai Museum: suitable for history, art and culture.
Nanjing Road: suitable for shopping and urban experience.

Food recommendations:
Xiaolongbao, shengjianbao, scallion oil noodles, local wontons and crab-based dishes.

Suggested route:
Day 1: The Bund, Nanjing Road, Lujiazui.
Day 2: Wukang Road, Xujiahui, Shanghai Museum.
Day 3: Yu Garden, Tianzifang, local food exploration.

Travel tips:
Avoid planning too many distant attractions in one day. Use metro for most urban travel.
