Chengdu Travel Guide

Chengdu is suitable for food, relaxed travel, pandas, tea houses, old streets and local lifestyle experience.

Recommended attractions:
Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding: best visited in the morning.
Kuanzhai Alley: suitable for old-town atmosphere and snacks.
People's Park: suitable for tea house culture and local lifestyle.
Wuhou Shrine: suitable for Three Kingdoms history.
Jinli Street: suitable for night views and food.
Taikoo Li: suitable for shopping, cafes and modern city experience.

Food recommendations:
Hot pot, chuanchuan, dan dan noodles, rabbit head, mapo tofu and local snacks.

Suggested route:
Day 1: Panda Base, Taikoo Li, Chunxi Road.
Day 2: People's Park, Kuanzhai Alley, Wuhou Shrine, Jinli.
Day 3: Food exploration and relaxed citywalk.

Travel tips:
Chengdu is suitable for a slower pace. Avoid scheduling spicy food for every meal if the traveler is not used to it.
